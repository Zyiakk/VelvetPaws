# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-15)

First version.

- Own cat model and skeleton, six coat patterns with free colours, eyes with pupils that widen and narrow.
- Walks, trots, runs, sits, lies (sphinx), sleeps, grooms, stretches, yawns, hisses, jumps up onto and down from
  furniture, rubs around Jodi's legs; tail and ears move on their own.
- Follows Jodi, waits, roams, comes when called; flees from enemies.
- Jodi's office is her home: 53 places (sofas, armchairs, bed, desk, window sills, shelf compartments); she leaves out
  places behind closed doors.
- Meows and purring from recordings; hiss, yawn and landing synthetic.
- AltUI page "VelvetPaws" with behaviour, look and sound settings, presets and quick menu actions.
