# VelvetPaws 0.1.0

A cat companion for *The Killing Antidote*. She follows Jodi, waits or roams, climbs onto furniture, window sills and
shelf compartments, sits, lies and sleeps there, purrs and meows, and now and then weaves around Jodi's legs. Jodi's
office is her home. Everything about her - model, skeleton, fur, animations - is built from scratch; meows and purring
come from real recordings.

## Installation

| File | Copy to |
|---|---|
| `TKA_BlueprintLoader.pak` ([Blueprint Loader](https://www.nexusmods.com/thekillingantidote/mods/994?tab=files), separate mod) | `TheKillingAntidote/Content/Paks/~mods/` (create the folder; the name starts with a tilde) |
| `VelvetPaws.pak` | `TheKillingAntidote/Mods/` |

AltUI is recommended: with it, the cat gets her own page in the Mods tab. Without AltUI she runs with the defaults
(active, following Jodi, grey tabby).

## Settings (AltUI, Mods tab, entry "VelvetPaws")

- **Behaviour:** on/off, mode (Follow, Wait, Roam), take along on missions, call her (button and key), how long she
  stays on a spot (minimum and maximum, 0.5-30 minutes).
- **Look:** coat (solid, tabby, brindle, spotted, tricolour, markings), base, second and eye colour, scale (40-115,
  small values make a kitten), name.
- **Sounds:** meow on/off and how often, purr on/off, volume.
- Presets for common coats; the quick menu (wheel) switches her mode.

## Known limits

- Petting is not in yet.
- Hissing, yawning and landing sounds are synthetic.
